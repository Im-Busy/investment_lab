## 🔑 Key Terminology & Visual Elements

| Term | Description (for text-only representation) |
|------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Resistance Line** | Horizontal/angled line where price repeatedly fails to break above |
| **Support Line** | Horizontal/angled line where price repeatedly finds buying interest |
| **Neckline** | Key support/resistance level in Head & Shoulders patterns; breakout confirms reversal |
| **Trendline** | Diagonal line connecting successive highs (downtrend) or lows (uptrend) |
| **Breakout** | Price movement beyond a defined boundary (resistance/support), signaling potential continuation/reversal |
| **Retest** | Price returning to test a broken level (now acting as support/resistance) after breakout |
| **Pullback** | Temporary counter-trend move within a larger trend |
| **Consolidation** | Period of sideways price movement before next directional move |
| **Target** | Projected price objective measured from pattern completion |

## 📈 Chart Patterns Catalog

### 1. Reversal Patterns
| Pattern Name | Visual Description | Key Components | Signal |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Double Top** | "M" shape: two peaks at similar height separated by a trough | Resistance line at peaks, neckline at trough | Bearish reversal after neckline break |
| **Double Bottom** | "W" shape: two troughs at similar depth separated by a peak | Support line at troughs, neckline at peak | Bullish reversal after neckline break |
| **Triple Top** | Three peaks at similar resistance level | Strong resistance line, neckline support | Strong bearish reversal |
| **Triple Bottom** | Three troughs at similar support level | Strong support line, neckline resistance | Strong bullish reversal |
| **Head and Shoulders** | Left shoulder (peak), higher head (peak), right shoulder (lower peak) | Neckline connecting troughs between shoulders | Bearish reversal on neckline break |
| **Inverse Head and Shoulders** | Mirror of H&S: left trough, lower head, right higher trough | Neckline connecting peaks | Bullish reversal on neckline break |
| **Rounding Top** | Gradual inverted "U" shape: slow rise then slow decline | Curved resistance, breakdown point | Bearish reversal |
| **Rounding Bottom** | Gradual "U" shape: slow decline then slow rise | Curved support, breakout point | Bullish reversal |
| **Island Reversal** | Price gap isolates a cluster of candles from prior/following price action | Gap before and after consolidation | Strong reversal signal |
| **V-Top / V-Bottom** | Sharp, near-vertical price reversal forming "V" or inverted "V" | No consolidation; abrupt momentum shift | Immediate reversal |

### 2. Continuation Patterns
| Pattern Name | Visual Description | Key Components | Signal |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Ascending Triangle** | Flat resistance top + rising support line (higher lows) | Horizontal resistance, ascending trendline | Bullish continuation (breakout up) |
| **Descending Triangle** | Flat support bottom + declining resistance (lower highs) | Horizontal support, descending trendline | Bearish continuation (breakdown down) |
| **Symmetrical Triangle** | Converging trendlines: lower highs + higher lows | Upper descending + lower ascending trendlines | Neutral; breakout direction determines bias |
| **Rising Wedge** | Converging upward-sloping trendlines; steeper support | Upper/lower trendlines both rising, support steeper | Typically bearish reversal/continuation |
| **Falling Wedge** | Converging downward-sloping trendlines; steeper resistance | Upper/lower trendlines both falling, resistance steeper | Typically bullish reversal/continuation |
| **Bullish Flag** | Sharp upward move (pole) + small downward-sloping consolidation | Parallel downward channel after strong rally | Bullish continuation |
| **Bearish Flag** | Sharp downward move (pole) + small upward-sloping consolidation | Parallel upward channel after strong drop | Bearish continuation |
| **Bullish Pennant** | Sharp rally + small symmetrical triangle consolidation | Small triangle after strong upward impulse | Bullish continuation |
| **Bearish Pennant** | Sharp drop + small symmetrical triangle consolidation | Small triangle after strong downward impulse | Bearish continuation |
| **Cup and Handle** | "U"-shaped cup + small downward drift (handle) near right rim | Rounded bottom, shallow pullback handle | Bullish continuation on handle breakout |
| **Rectangle (Bullish/Bearish)** | Horizontal support and resistance forming a box | Parallel horizontal lines | Breakout direction confirms continuation |

### 3. Complex & Advanced Patterns
| Pattern Name | Visual Description | Key Components | Signal |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Diamond Top** | Price expands then contracts forming diamond shape | Widening then narrowing volatility | Bearish reversal |
| **Diamond Bottom** | Inverted diamond: contraction then expansion | Narrowing then widening volatility | Bullish reversal |
| **Broadening Top/Bottom** | Diverging trendlines forming megaphone shape | Increasing volatility, higher highs + lower lows | Often reversal; high uncertainty |
| **Pipe Top / Pipe Bottom** | Two sharp, narrow price spikes in opposite directions | Volatile reversal candles with wicks | Short-term reversal signal |
| **Spikes** | Single candle with long wick and small body | Extreme rejection at price level | Potential reversal or exhaustion |
| **Gaps** | Price "jumps" with no trading between sessions | Empty space on chart between candles | Continuation or reversal depending on context |
| **Broadening Channel** | Parallel but diverging trendlines | Price oscillates with increasing amplitude | Volatility expansion; caution advised |

### 4. Harmonic & Wave-Based Patterns
| Pattern Name | Visual Description | Key Components | Signal |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Harmonic Patterns** | Geometric price structures based on Fibonacci ratios (e.g., Gartley, Bat, Butterfly) | Precise retracement/extensions (0.618, 1.272, etc.) | High-probability reversal zones |
| **Elliott Wave** | Impulsive 5-wave move + corrective 3-wave cycle | Wave degrees, Fibonacci relationships | Trend forecasting framework |
| **Three Drives** | Three consecutive impulsive moves with corrective pullbacks | Symmetrical wave structure, Fibonacci alignment | Reversal at completion of 3rd drive |
| **Quasimodo** | Modified H&S with asymmetrical shoulders and neckline break | False breakout, then reversal | High-probability reversal |
| **Bump and Run** | Initial trend (lead-in), acceleration (bump), then reversal (run) | Trendline break after parabolic move | Reversal after exhaustion |

### 5. Miscellaneous Patterns
| Pattern Name | Visual Description | Key Components | Signal |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Dead Cat Bounce** | Sharp decline → small temporary rally → continuation down | Weak retracement within strong downtrend | Bearish continuation (trap for bulls) |
| **Scallop Pattern** | Series of rounded bottoms with ascending peaks | "J"-shaped curves repeating upward | Bullish continuation |
| **Ascending/Descending Staircase** | Series of higher lows (ascending) or lower highs (descending) with horizontal pauses | Step-like price progression | Trend continuation |
| **Impulsive Move Pattern** | Strong, near-vertical price movement with minimal pullback | High momentum, low volatility during move | Trend strength confirmation |

## 🎨 Illustration Descriptions (For Text-Only AI)

Since the original PDF contains visual diagrams, here is a textual description of how each illustration is typically structured:

1. **Price Axis**: Vertical axis represents price; horizontal axis represents time.
2. **Candlesticks/Bars**: Each pattern is drawn using Japanese candlesticks or OHLC bars showing open, high, low, close.
3. **Reference Lines**:
   - *Solid lines*: Actual support/resistance or trendlines drawn from price points.
   - *Dashed lines*: Projected targets, neckline extensions, or retest levels.
4. **Labels & Annotations**:
   - Arrows indicate breakout direction.
   - "W", "M", "N", "V", "A" symbols mark pattern shapes.
   - "Target" labels show measured move projections.
   - "Break Out", "Retest", "Pullback" mark key action points.
5. **Pattern Stages**: Many illustrations show:
   - Formation phase (price building the pattern)
   - Confirmation point (break of neckline/trendline)
   - Post-breakout behavior (retest, continuation)
6. **Color Coding** (inferred):
   - Green/upward arrows for bullish signals
   - Red/downward arrows for bearish signals
   - Neutral colors for consolidation phases
