## Patterns Identified

### Reversal Patterns

| Pattern | Direction | Key Trigger | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Head & Shoulders | Bearish | Break below neckline | Three peaks: L-shoulder, higher head, R-shoulder |
| Inverted H&S | Bullish | Break above neckline | Three troughs mirror of H&S |
| Double Top (M) | Bearish | Break below trough between peaks | Two equal highs, buyers lose interest |
| Double Bottom (W) | Bullish | Break above peak between troughs | Two equal lows, trend reversal |
| Rounding Bottom (Saucer) | Bullish | Gradual U-shape on weekly chart | Long consolidation transitioning bearish→bullish |

### Continuation Patterns

| Pattern | Direction | Key Trigger | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Cup & Handle | Bullish | Break above handle | U-shaped cup + pullback handle (~1/3 retracement) |
| Symmetric Triangle | Neutral | Break above/below converging lines | Lower highs + higher lows |
| Ascending Triangle | Bullish | Break above flat resistance | Higher lows converging to flat top |
| Descending Triangle | Bearish | Break below flat support | Lower highs converging to flat bottom |
| Flag (Parallelogram) | Continuation | Break in flagpole direction | Sharp pole + small parallel consolidation |

### Gap Patterns

| Pattern | Context | Volume Signal |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Breakaway Gap | Out of congestion/range | Requires enthusiasm, many more buyers/sellers |
| Runaway (Measuring) Gap | Mid-trend acceleration | Panic buying, traders who missed entry |
| Exhaustion Gap | Near end of trend | Exceptionally high volume, trend reversal soon |
